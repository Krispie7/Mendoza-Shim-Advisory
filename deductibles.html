Understanding coverage

What does your deductible actually mean?

A plain-language guide to deductibles, copays, coinsurance and out-of-pocket costs.

← All resources

What a deductible is

A deductible is an amount you may pay for covered services before your plan begins paying for certain benefits. Some services may be covered before the deductible is met.

Other costs matter

A copayment is a fixed amount for a covered service. Coinsurance is a percentage of the allowed cost. Premiums are what you pay to maintain coverage, regardless of whether you use it.

Look beyond the deductible

An out-of-pocket maximum, where applicable, limits certain covered expenses under the policy. Premiums, noncovered services and some out-of-network costs generally do not count. Review the plan documents to understand which costs apply.

General educational information only. Actual benefits, costs and eligibility depend on the policy and circumstances. Review official plan documents and applicable rules.
