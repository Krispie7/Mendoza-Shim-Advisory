Understanding private and Marketplace coverage

How different types of coverage can vary in eligibility, benefits and consumer protections.

← All resources

Private and Marketplace are not opposites

Marketplace plans are offered by private insurers through a government-run or state-run exchange. Plans sold outside the Marketplace can also be private insurance. The type of policy and its protections matter more than the label.

What can differ

ACA-compliant individual major medical coverage generally covers pre-existing conditions and essential health benefits. Other types of policies may use medical underwriting, exclude certain conditions, limit benefits or have different renewal rules. Premium tax credits are generally available only through the Marketplace, subject to eligibility.

Questions worth asking

Is the policy ACA-compliant? What is excluded? How are prescriptions, networks and hospital care handled? What would I pay in a high-cost year? Read the actual policy documents before making a decision.
