Self-employment

Health insurance when you work for yourself

Questions to ask when evaluating coverage outside of an employer plan.

← All resources

Start with your circumstances

Without employer-sponsored benefits, consider how your household income, health needs, location and preferred providers affect your choices.

Compare available paths

Marketplace coverage, qualifying coverage through a spouse, and other individual insurance options may be worth exploring. Marketplace financial assistance depends on eligibility. Some private policies may use medical underwriting or offer more limited benefits.

Evaluate the full picture

Compare premiums, deductibles, networks, prescriptions, exclusions and potential out-of-pocket expenses. Ask about enrollment windows and avoid canceling existing coverage until you understand when replacement coverage begins.

General educational information only. Actual benefits, costs and eligibility depend on the policy and circumstances. Review official plan documents and applicable rules.
