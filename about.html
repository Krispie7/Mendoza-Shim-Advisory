I’m Kristian Mendoza-Shim, and I believe people deserve to understand what they’re purchasing, what their coverage actually provides, and whether it makes sense for their circumstances.

I work with individuals, families, self-employed professionals and business owners to navigate health coverage options. When another solution is more appropriate, I can help identify next steps or connect you with a referral partner.

My approach is to listen first, explain the details in plain language, and give you room to make your own decision.
